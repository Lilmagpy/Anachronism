## What the lead found playing the game headlessly (2026-11-01)
A scripted run of `rome_264`, `rome_509` and `rome_49` for 4-12 turns each (one turn = two years):
- **Only 11 of 482 years have real magistrates.** The pack's `fasti.yaml` is a sample (509-500,
  264-260). In 264 BC the client shows consuls L. Postumius Megellus and M'. Valerius Maximus
  Messalla (age 81); the real consuls were Ap. Claudius Caudex and M. Fulvius Flaccus, who the
  sample lists as censors. From 258 BC every office is filled by weighted draw from 19 gentes and
  13 sample people, so 49 BC elects "Postumus Cominius Auruncus" and "Spurius Larcius Rufus".
  A complete draft of every year 509-27 BC (475 years, 161 gentes, sources and confidence per
  year, gap years for the anarchy and dictator years) is on branch `origin/claude/r1-fasti` at
  `data/rome/fasti.yaml` and `data/rome/fasti_gaps.yaml`. Its gens ids are spelled with a
  doubled i (`corneliii`): map them.
- **Bugs:** the same man elected to both consulships (244 BC) and consul + praetor in one year
  (240 BC); a legendary dead man "re-elected" (Brutus, consul 506 and 504 BC: generated
  namesakes take a dead man's cognomen); consuls aged 81.
- **Senate forecasts are meaningless:** every proposal forecasts yes 58%, no 0%, because gentes
  carry one stance (`war`) or none. 16-30 proposals are offered, including war on Parthia in
  509 BC.
- **Adopted ideas never show in the city.** `engine/city.py` records anachronisms with `builds`
  (`road`, `dome`, `aqueduct`...), the `city` view lists them, nothing in `client/scripts/urbs/`
  reads them.
- The ideas library is the old generic one (108 nodes: crossbow, postal relay, water wheel,
  citizens' assembly) dated for the world, not for Rome; advisers are "Grand Steward", "Court
  Diviner". Dilemmas invent "Publius Claudius Caudex".
- Allies, Latin rights, citizenship, colonies (the `colony:` proposal only sets a flag),
  provinces and governors, the grain supply, coinage, land and debt, auspices, prodigies and
  cults, the three army systems and census-class recruitment: in the plan (R3, R4), not built.
- Working: six starts load; two consuls a year; elections, deaths, census; Senate votes with a
  tribune; city events (games, munera, dedications, senate, triumph) reach Urbs; armies and
  fleets fight; chapters and dilemmas fire.

## Shared contract for this round
- Five agents in parallel, each in its own worktree and branch. **Edit only your files.**
  Shared files (`engine/turn.py`, `engine/republic.py` hooks, `tools/view.py`,
  `content/schema/__init__.py`, `content/schema/pack.py`, `content/loader.py`, `client/scripts/
  main.gd`, `hud.gd`, `urbs/urbs.gd`) belong to the lead unless your task names them: put your
  yearly rule in your own module as `run_year(state, events, year)` (or `run_turn`), your view
  in your own `tools/<name>_view.py`, your content kinds in your own schema file, and write in
  your report the exact one-line hooks the lead must add. Where you must touch a shared file,
  keep the edit to a marked block (`# F1 <your task>`) of a few lines.
- Determinism: all randomness via `engine.rng.GameRng` from the state; dicts iterated in
  sorted-id order; integers (basis points) only. Every new rule has a test; a sim over several
  seeds must keep the invariants (`tests/engine/test_invariants*.py` show how).
- Every historical fact cites its source in the YAML (`sources:`), `# unverified` when the
  passage could not be read. LacusCurtius is often down (503): Perseus, Gutenberg (Livy), the
  Latin Library, Wikisource and archive.org (Platner-Ashby) work. Wikipedia may be used for
  dates and lists of office-holders (facts, attributed), never copied as text.

  briefs/urbs.md); `--smoke` must keep printing SMOKE OK; parse-check every script you touch.
- Commit in small batches and **push after every commit** (`git push -u origin <branch>`).
