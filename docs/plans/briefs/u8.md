## The gap, concretely
| Rome Reborn | Ours today |
|---|---|
| Deep blue sky, hard sun, crisp shadows, subtle aerial haze with depth | Grey-pink wash, overexposed, flat lighting, fog like soup |
| Marble with veining and sheen, travertine, brick with mortar, painted stucco, terracotta tiles with ridges, bronze and gilt | Flat colours or one noise pattern; black holes for doors and windows |
| Temples with fluted columns, real capitals, entablature with mouldings, pediment sculpture, acroteria, gilded details, bronze doors, podium steps | Box with a flat brick texture and a pitched roof; the Forum drawn as a sunken courtyard ringed by one continuous orange roof |
| Travertine slab paving in the Forum; honorific columns and statues; the Rostra with beaks | Sand |
| The Circus in a real valley between the Palatine and Aventine, the Capitol towering, retaining walls, ramps and steps where streets climb | Flat tan rectangle on flat ground; houses sitting on bare slopes |
| Umbrella pines, cypresses, plane trees, gardens | No trees inside the city; farms as bright green striped stickers |
| People everywhere (the main cue for scale), carts, animals, awnings, stalls | Empty streets |
| Tile roofs with ridge tiles and antefixes; shuttered windows; balconies with railings | Flat roof slabs; laundry as coloured squares stuck on walls |

## Shared contract
- Materials: `client/scripts/urbs/materials.gd` (class `UrbsMaterials`). Call
  `UrbsMaterials.get_material("marble_luna")` etc. The name list is in the file header and is
  stable. The rendering agent replaces the insides with PBR shaders; everyone else only uses
  the names (and may add a name to the list, with a plain-colour fallback, if a surface is
  missing).
- Figures: `client/scripts/urbs/spectacles/figure.gd` + `rig.gd` (skinned, two LODs) and
  `client/scripts/urbs/crowd.gd` (MultiMesh crowds). Reuse them for any people.
- Performance budget stays: 60 fps at 1600x900 on a laptop GPU; measure draw calls and
  triangles with the preview `--perf` flags where they exist; MultiMesh and LOD for anything
  repeated.
- Edit only the files your task names. Five agents run in parallel. `main.gd`, `hud.gd`,
  `project.godot` belong to nobody this round: ask in your report if you need a hook.
- Screenshots: take the SAME five views before and after (the camera flags are in
  `urbs.gd`'s header): `--urbs --year=-44 --mode=street --at=60,20,30 --yaw=300 --pitch=-10
  --hour=9` (Forum towards the Capitol), `--at=aedes_saturni` (Saturn from its door),
  `--at=130,420,40 --yaw=200 --pitch=-15` (Circus from the Palatine), `--at=10,20,1.7 --yaw=255`
  (Forum at eye height, towards Saturn and the Tabularium), `--mode=city --at=50,-50,700` (from the air). Look at them, compare to
  the reference, iterate until the gap in YOUR row of the table is closed. Report the paths.
- Commit in small batches on your branch in your worktree and **push after every commit**
  (`git push -u origin <branch>`). Never push any other branch.
