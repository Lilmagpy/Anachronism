# The Warring States, 350 BC — sources of the scenario and what is uncertain

The scenario (`src/anachronism/content/packs/east_asia/`) was written by Claude from general
historical knowledge (standard histories of the Warring States: the *Shiji* tradition as
summarised in modern surveys such as the Cambridge History of Ancient China). It aims to be
plausible and recognisable, not a scholarly reconstruction. Please correct anything wrong.

## Fairly solid
- The seven great states and their capitals in 350 BC: Qin (moving to Xianyang this year),
  Wei (Daliang, moved from Anyi in the 360s), Zhao (Handan), Han (Xinzheng), Qi (Linzi),
  Yan (Ji), Chu (Ying).
- Smaller states still independent: the Zhou royal domain (Luoyang), Song, Lu, Wey,
  Zhongshan, Yue (ruling from Wu), Shu and Ba (conquered by Qin only in 316 BC), and the
  Yiqu Rong (conquered by Qin in 272 BC).
- Wei holds Hexi and the Upper Commandery west of the Yellow River (lost to Qin 330–328 BC).
- Technology: iron tools and cast iron spreading; crossbows in use; the Hong Canal (Wei);
  the Jixia Academy (Qi); Shen Buhai's reforms in Han (from 351 BC).

## Estimates and simplifications
- **Populations** are rough: about 27 million in the Zhou states in total, divided by the
  size and wealth of each state. Ancient figures are very uncertain.
- **Borders** are grown by the game from each province's centre over the real terrain
  (D-056); they are about right, not exact.
- **Hanzhong** is given to Shu (Shu took Nanzheng from Qin in 387 BC; control after that is
  unclear).
- **Gojoseon**: where its centre lay in the 4th century BC (Liaodong or the Taedong valley) is
  debated; the scenario gives it both.
- **Frontier peoples** (Donghu, Linhu, Loufan, Yuezhi, Qiang) are simplified to one province
  each; the Xiongnu are not yet named in the sources at this date.
- **Beyond the Zhou world** (Korea's south, Kyushu, Kinai, the Min and Lingnan Yue, the Red
  River delta, Dian) are unclaimed lands with small populations.
- India and Central Asia are on the map but not yet in the scenario.
