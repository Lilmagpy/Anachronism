# Europe in the Year 1000 — sources of the scenario and what is uncertain

Written by Claude from general historical knowledge (standard modern surveys of the early
medieval West, Byzantium and the Islamic world). Plausible and recognisable, not a scholarly
reconstruction. Please correct anything wrong.

## Fairly solid
- Rulers in 1000: Æthelred II (England), Robert II (France), Richard II (Normandy),
  Otto III (Holy Roman Empire), Sylvester II (pope, formerly Gerbert of Aurillac), Pietro II
  Orseolo (doge of Venice), Sweyn Forkbeard (Denmark), Olaf Tryggvason (Norway, killed at
  Svolder in September 1000), Olof Skötkonung (Sweden), Bolesław I (Poland; the Congress of
  Gniezno, March 1000), Stephen I (Hungary, crowned about 1000), Vladimir the Great (Kievan
  Rus'), Basil II (Byzantium, at war with Samuel of Bulgaria), al-Hakim (Fatimid caliph),
  Mahmud of Ghazni, Alfonso V (León), Gagik I (Armenia).
- Córdoba was governed by the chamberlain al-Mansur in the name of Caliph Hisham II until
  al-Mansur's death in 1002; the Buyid emirs ruled Baghdad over the Abbasid caliph.

## Estimates and simplifications
- **Populations** are rough.
- **Brian Boru** was king of Munster and became High King of Ireland in 1002.
- **Kenneth III** of Scotland, **Boleslaus III** of Bohemia, **Badis ibn Mansur** of the
  Zirids, **Ja'far al-Kalbi** of Sicily, **Bagrat III** of Georgia (then king of Abkhazia,
  uniting Georgia by 1008), **Baha al-Dawla** (Buyid) and **Ramon Borrell** of Barcelona
  are placed by the usual dates; Navarre's and Makuria's rulers are left blank.
- France's great duchies (Aquitaine, Burgundy, Toulouse, Flanders…) are shown as French
  provinces although their lords were nearly independent; Normandy is shown separately.
- Wales, Brittany, the Lombard principalities, Croatia, Serbia, the Baltic and Finnic
  peoples, the Pechenegs, Volga Bulgaria and the Jazira are unclaimed lands.
