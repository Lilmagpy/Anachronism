# Alexander's inheritance, 336 BC — sources of the scenario and what is uncertain

The scenario (`src/anachronism/content/packs/europe/`, files ending `_336`, and
`scenarios/alexander.yaml`) was written by Claude from general historical knowledge
(standard surveys of Philip II and Alexander, the Achaemenid empire, and the western
Mediterranean in the fourth century BC). It aims to be plausible and recognisable, not a
scholarly reconstruction. Please correct anything wrong.

## Fairly solid
- The moment: Philip II was murdered at Aegae in 336 BC at the wedding of his daughter
  Cleopatra to Alexander I of Epirus; his son Alexander, twenty, took the throne.
- Darius III became Great King the same year, after the poisonings of Artaxerxes III and
  Arses by the vizier Bagoas.
- Philip's advance force under Parmenion and Attalus had already crossed into Asia.
- Thebes revolted in 335 BC and was destroyed; the Illyrians under Cleitus and Glaucias
  rose the same year; Sparta stayed out of the League of Corinth, and Agis III fought
  Antipater in 331 BC and died at Megalopolis.
- The Odrysian kingdom paid tribute to Macedon; Seuthes III later rebelled.
- Alexander of Epirus crossed to Italy (334 BC) at the call of Taras.
- Carthage and Syracuse had fought over Sicily for a century; Timoleon died about 337 BC.
- Rome was in the Samnite wars (the first 343-341 BC, the second from 326 BC); the treaty
  of 348 BC with Carthage divided their spheres.

## Estimates and simplifications
- **Populations** are rough: the Achaemenid empire perhaps 17 million in the provinces
  shown (the eastern satrapies beyond Parthia are left off the map); Macedon with Thessaly
  and the Thracian coast about 1.4 million.
- **Mobilisation** (`martial_bp`): Macedon counts four times the usual share of its people
  under arms (Philip's standing army); Persia under half, since most subjects of the Great
  King were never levied. These are game numbers, tuned so Macedon and Persia start roughly
  level.
- **Thebes, Syracuse, Carthage, the Scythians and Rome** have no named leader in 336 BC in
  the game. Rome's consuls changed each year; Carthage's suffetes are poorly known.
- **Faiths**: one "Olympian gods" faith for all Greeks and Macedonians, not spreading;
  Zoroastrianism for the Persian court (spreading, as the official faith of an empire);
  local gods elsewhere. Egyptian, Babylonian and Phoenician cults inside Persia are folded
  into Persia's faith for simplicity.
- **Links by hand**: Persis to Media, Media to Parthia and Armenia, Armenia to Cappadocia
  and Pontus, Syria to Nabataea, and the Strait of Messina between Syracuse and Magna
  Graecia.
- **Emblem**: Persia's banner shows ΠΕΡ, as the Greeks wrote the name; Old Persian cuneiform is not in the game's font.
